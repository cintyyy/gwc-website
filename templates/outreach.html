What We Offer

Hands-on learning,
real projects

Our sessions at Graham Middle School are designed to be accessible and fun — no prior experience needed. We guide students through real coding concepts and help them build something they're proud of.

🌐
HTML & Web Structure

Students learn how websites are built with HTML — from basic tags and headings to links, images, and page structure. They create their own "About Me" pages, adding text, styling, and formatting elements like bold, italic, and color.

🎨
CSS & Visual Styling

Students discover how to make their pages look great using CSS — changing fonts, colors, backgrounds, and text alignment. They learn how style properties work and practice making design choices that reflect their personality.

⚡
JavaScript & Interactivity

Students bring their pages to life with JavaScript — creating buttons, animations, and interactive programs. They explore variables, functions, loops, and conditionals through activities like a car animation and a number guessing game.

🐍
Python Programming

Students get introduced to Python, one of the most beginner-friendly and widely used programming languages. Through hands-on exercises they practice core programming concepts in a new environment.

🎮
Final Projects

Students apply everything they've learned to build a complete project from scratch, showcasing their creativity and problem-solving skills.
